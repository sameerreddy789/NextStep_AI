# NextStep AI UX Research Plan

## Objectives
- Validate the "Interview-Aware" workflow: Does it effectively encourage users to validate their resume?
- Identify friction points in the Resume Upload → Interview → Roadmap journey.
- Evaluate the clarity and motivational impact of the 0-100 Readiness Score.
- Understand the needs of the 13+ language segment for personalized roadmaps.

## Target User Segments
1. **The Overwhelmed Student**: Struggles with what to study, lacks company-specific context.
2. **The Anxious Fresher**: Freezes during live interactions, needs a safe space to practice.
3. **The Pivot Professional (Career Switcher)**: Needs a fast, structured bridge between current skills and target role.

## Methodologies
1. **Competitive Analysis**: Benchmarking against LeetCode, Interviewing.io, and LinkedIn.
2. **User Journey Mapping**: Documenting emotional highs/lows in the current MVP flow.
3. **Heuristic Evaluation**: Expert review of the interface for accessibility, clarity, and consistency.
4. **Analytics Strategy**: Defining event-based tracking for conversion funnel analysis.

## Success Metrics
- **Interview Conversion**: % of users who start an interview after resume analysis.
- **Task Stickiness**: % of users who complete at least 3 roadmap tasks in the first week.
- **Score Understanding**: Users can correctly explain what their Readiness Score means and how to improve it.
- **Time to Value**: How long it takes from landing to a personalized roadmap.

## Research Questions
- Is the "Resume Analysis" result screen persuasive enough to trigger an interview?
- Do users feel "heard" by the AI during the behavioral interview feedback?
- Is the Roadmap too daunting? (6 weeks vs. immediate actions).
- How do non-English speakers experience the language-aware features?
